HOW WE WORK. There are three sheet-and-script loops and I make every judgement call in a
sheet. You never put an answer in a sheet for me — not yes/no, not a coordinate, not a
label. You run the scripts, rebuild, verify, and report.

  R/coord_review.R    data/coord_review.xlsx    coordinates
  R/label_review.R    data/label_review.xlsx    label merges
  R/absent_review.R   data/absent_review.xlsx   ABSENT affiliations
  Three read-only sweeps feed the first two: R/check_coord_sanity.R (the country an
  affiliation names), R/check_coord_place.R (the town it names), R/check_label_candidates.R
  (two labels that may be one place).

What each sheet takes:
  coord_review.xlsx   column A: yes / no / absent / checked. yes takes the coordinate in H
                      and I; `absent` leaves the label without one and appends an
                      accept_as_is carrying my note from M; `checked` is for a row the town
                      sweep flagged that is right anyway — it keeps the coordinate and
                      appends the accept_as_is that stops the sweep raising it. Six cells are
                      read: A, C, H, I, M, N. `place_check` (S) says what town the point is
                      actually in; `geocoder_notes` is V.
  label_review.xlsx   column A: yes / no. Column C `keep` names the survivor and may name
                      any label — or `new: <name>` to create one. Four cells are read: A, C,
                      R, S. `warning` is Q, the two maps links W and X, `lake` Y.
