HOW WE WORK. There are four sheet-and-script loops and I make every judgement call in a
sheet. You never put an answer in a sheet for me — not yes/no, not a coordinate, not a
label. You run the scripts, rebuild, verify, and report.

  R/coord_review.R    data/coord_review.xlsx    coordinates
  R/label_review.R    data/label_review.xlsx    label merges
  R/absent_review.R   data/absent_review.xlsx   ABSENT affiliations
  R/check_coord_sanity.R and R/check_label_candidates.R are read-only sweeps that feed
  the first two.

What each sheet takes:
  coord_review.xlsx   column A: yes / no / absent. yes takes the coordinate in H and I;
                      `absent` leaves the label without one and appends an accept_as_is
                      decision carrying my note from M. Six cells are read: A, C, H, I, M, N.
  label_review.xlsx   column A: yes / no. Column C `keep` names the survivor and may name
                      any label — or `new: <name>` to create one, which is how DRASS Reunion
                      was made. Four cells are read: A, C, R, S. The `warning` column is Q
                      (CLAUDE.md says O; it is wrong) and the two maps links are W and X.

Run label_review.R before coord_review.R when both are in play: a merge patches the
coordinate sheet and coord_review.R turns that patch back into decisions.
